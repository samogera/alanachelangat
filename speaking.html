Speaking for children on the world stage.

Keynotes, UN climate summits and youth assemblies across three countries, with one message: protecting children and protecting the planet are the same mission.

Send a speaking request

The climate crisis is also a child rights crisis.

At the 3rd International Conference on Child Protection in Africa, themed “Child Protection and Climate Change”, Alana stood alongside leaders and experts from across the continent and called for a Child Protection and Climate Finance Compact:

  1. 01Real seats for children and youth at decision-making tables
  2. 02Schools that are safe, climate-resilient spaces
  3. 03At least 10% of climate finance for child protection and youth-led innovation
  4. 04Technology that protects families before crises become disasters

Hosted by Kenya’s State Department for Children Services, Ministry of Gender, Culture and Children Services.
